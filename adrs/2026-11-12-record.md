# 2026-11-12 - `record`: habits week by week against a baseline

## Status

Accepted

## Context

`report`, `insights` and `live` say where the money went and what to change.
Nothing said whether the changes worked: a user could not see if this week's
habits were better than last month's. Raw $ from week to week mostly measures
how much work was done, so it cannot judge a habit.

## Decision

- Records are back-to-back periods of local days (7 by default, `--days`),
  started and closed by the refresher with no command. Record #1 starts on the
  first refresh after upgrade; the 7 days before it are frozen as the baseline.
- A record starts or closes only after an index pass with nothing behind.
- Each closed record is frozen (`records.json`, newest 52). The screen compares
  the live record with the baseline (verdict) and with the record before (table).
- Five habits are scored, with the `live` advisor's aims: $ per MTok of new
  work, avoidable $ share, cache reads, re-sending, re-reading. Raw $, prompts,
  $/prompt, commands and active time are shown as volume, never scored.
- A change under 3% ($) or 2 points (shares) is `≈`; before day 3 nothing is
  scored.
- Each record gets a focus: the top rule of the record (or baseline) before it,
  R6 and R8 excepted. The record shows if its share of $ went down.
- Prompts, interrupts, `/clear`, `/model` and compactions are counted per day in
  the index (`index/habits.json`); `PRICES_VERSION` `.2` rebuilds it once so the
  baseline has them.
- The statusline shows `» record #N done …` until `cc-spend record` runs.

## Options considered

| Option                                | Why not / why                                                       |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `record start` / `record stop`        | The user asked for no extra step; a forgotten start records nothing |
| Baseline after one week of recording  | A week of waiting; the index already holds the days before          |
| Score raw $ or $/prompt               | Rewards doing less work, not better habits                          |
| A live statusline part for the record | Noise every render; one note when a record closes is enough         |
| Count habits in the render            | The render never reads transcripts                                  |

## Consequences

- The first refresh after upgrade re-reads every transcript once (unbounded).
- Prompt detection depends on transcript lines not in the public docs; a format
  change makes the counts drop to 0 without a self-check.
- A session counts (sessions, active time) in the record of its last activity.
- Days whose transcripts are gone keep their rows and counts from the re-price
  backup.
